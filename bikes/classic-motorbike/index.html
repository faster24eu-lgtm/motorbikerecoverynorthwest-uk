To shows and rallies

If you’d rather not ride a precious bike 80 miles to a show, we can deliver it and bring it back. Tell us the date, the venue and when you need it there. Book well ahead in the summer show season.

Restorations and barn finds

A bike that’s been in a shed for twenty years may have seized wheels, flat tyres, no brakes and no key. That’s fine. We can winch, skate or carry it onto the truck, and take it to your workshop or restorer. Tell us whether the wheels turn and whether it’s complete, and send a photo if you can.

Breaking down on the road

Classics do break down. Magnetos, points, carburettors and wiring all fail on long runs. If it’s let you down, we’ll get you and the bike home.

We strap classics with soft loops on strong points, never on old, thin bars or tired rubber, and we keep oily bikes off clean decks with a drip tray.
