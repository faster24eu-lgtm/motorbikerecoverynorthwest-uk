Heavy touring bike recovery

Loading a tourer

Most heavy tourers can be loaded with their luggage on, and we prefer it, as it saves you unpacking at the roadside. The fairings are wide and expensive, so straps go on the frame and lower triple clamp, never across plastic. Some tourers have a reverse gear or electric reverse; tell us if it still works, as it makes positioning easier.

Dropped it?

A heavy tourer on its side is hard to lift without hurting yourself or the bike. Don’t try to lift it alone. Turn off the fuel and ignition, get yourself safe, and let us know it’s down. We can lift it, check for leaks and load it.

On a long trip

Tourers break down a long way from home. If you’re touring the Lakes or heading for Scotland, we can take the bike to a local dealer, or all the way home. You hear the price first.

FAQ

Rider questions

Do I need to take my luggage off before you load the bike?

Usually not. Hard cases and top boxes can stay on. Take valuables with you.

Can you lift a dropped Gold Wing?

Yes. Tell us it’s down when you call, and don’t try to lift it on your own.
