Getting to Aintree

The circuit is off the A59 and close to Switch Island and the M57 and M58. It’s an easy run from Liverpool, Crosby and Bootle, Southport and the Wirral. On race days at the racecourse, the roads around Aintree are very busy, so check the date.

Sprints and track days

Aintree is popular for sprint events as well as track days. Sprint bikes are often stripped down, with no lights, no plates and sometimes no road-legal parts at all. That’s no problem for us: the bike travels on the truck.

If you come off

Call us from the paddock. We’ll collect the bike in whatever state it’s in and take it home or to a repairer. If you’re going to claim on track-day insurance, keep our invoice.

Yes. It travels on the truck, strapped down properly, so it doesn’t need to be road legal.
