Close to home for most of the North West

Three Sisters is just off the M6 between junctions 24 and 25, so it’s a short run from Wigan, St Helens, Leigh, Bolton and Warrington. That makes it ideal for riders who don’t want to ride a road bike there and back on track-day tyres.

First track day?

If you’re new to track days, having the bike delivered means one less thing to think about. You arrive fresh, the bike is waiting, and if you have a get-off on your first day, getting home isn’t a problem. We see a lot of small crashes at Three Sisters: bent levers, broken pegs, scuffed fairings. Most bikes can’t be ridden home afterwards, but they’re easy to collect.

Supermoto and minibikes

Three Sisters also runs supermoto and minibike days. We carry those too, along with spares and a stand if you need them.
