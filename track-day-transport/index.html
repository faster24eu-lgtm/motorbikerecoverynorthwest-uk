Track days

Track bike there. Track bike back.

Track-only bikes can’t be ridden on the road, and nobody wants to ride 60 miles home after a day of hard laps. We take your bike to the circuit, and bring it back, even if it’s in a few more pieces than when it left.

Circuits we run to

  • Oulton Park (near Tarporley, Cheshire), the big one for North West bike days and British Superbikes.
  • Aintree Circuit (Liverpool), a short, fast club circuit around the racecourse.
  • Three Sisters (Wigan), a tight, technical track, great for first track days and training.
  • Anglesey Circuit / Trac Môn (North Wales), along the A55 from Chester.

We also run further afield: Cadwell, Donington, Brands, Knockhill. Just ask.

How it works

  1. Book a drop-off time that gets you to the circuit before sign-on and noise testing.
  2. We collect from your home or garage. Spare wheels, tyre warmers, a paddock stand and a fuel can can usually come too. Tell us what you’re bringing.
  3. We collect at the end of the day, or the second you call if you bin it.

If it goes wrong on track

Crashed bikes can’t be ridden home and often can’t be rolled. Our operator can recover a crashed track bike from the paddock with a winch or dolly. Book transport home in advance, and we’ll still bring it back if it’s bent.

FAQ

Rider questions

Can you transport a race bike with no number plate or lights?

Yes. The bike is carried, not ridden, so it doesn’t need to be road legal.

Can you bring my tyre warmers, stands and spare wheels?

Usually, yes. Tell us when you book so there’s room on the truck.
