The Trough · recovery

Bike trouble on the Trough of Bowland?

The road through the Trough of Bowland runs from Dunsop Bridge to Lancaster through the Forest of Bowland: narrow, gated in places, with cattle grids, sheep and long stretches with no phone signal.

The road

It’s a slow, technical ride rather than a fast one: blind crests, gravel, and the odd cattle grid on a bend. Lovely on a dry day, slippery after rain.

What usually goes wrong here

Low-speed drops on gravel or a cattle grid, punctures, and bikes that won’t restart after stopping for photos. The biggest problem is usually getting signal to call.

If you’re stuck right now

Note the last landmark: Dunsop Bridge, the Trough summit, Marshaw or Abbeystead. Walk to higher ground for signal if you have to. A what3words address is perfect here.

  1. Get off the road, and get the bike off it too if you can.
  2. Send your location: a WhatsApp live pin, what3words, or the last landmark you passed.
  3. Call 020 8058 0013, hear the price, and we’ll send a bike-ready truck.

FAQ

Rider questions

How long does it take to get a recovery truck to the Trough of Bowland?

It depends on where the nearest available truck is and the traffic. Remote roads take longer than towns. We give you an honest estimate on the call.

What if I have no phone signal?

Walk to higher ground or the nearest village. A single bar is enough for a text or WhatsApp pin. Note the last landmark you passed.
