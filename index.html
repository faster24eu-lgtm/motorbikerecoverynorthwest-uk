How it works

Four steps, no membership

  1. Send your location. A WhatsApp pin, what3words or the nearest junction or lay-by.
  2. Hear the full price. Local recoveries start from £125. Nothing is booked until you say yes.
  3. A bike-ready truck comes out. Wheel chock, soft loops and proper ratchet straps. We text you the operator’s details.
  4. Bike delivered where you choose. Home, dealer, garage, track or storage, and usually you ride along in the cab.

FAQ

Rider questions

How much does motorbike recovery cost in the North West?

Local recoveries start from £125. Longer distances, night calls and difficult recoveries (a crashed bike, a remote pass) cost more. You always hear the full price before anyone is sent.

Do I need breakdown cover?

No. There’s no membership: you pay for this recovery only.

How will my bike be secured?

Front wheel in a chock, soft loops around the bars, yokes or frame, and ratchet straps pulling down and forward. Nothing goes across the tank, seat or fairings.

Can you take me as well as the bike?

Usually, yes. Most trucks have room for the rider, often a pillion too. Tell us when you call.
