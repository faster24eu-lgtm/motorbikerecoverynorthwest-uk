Before you pay

  • Check the MOT history on GOV.UK, using the registration number.
  • Check the frame and engine numbers match the V5C.
  • Consider a history check for outstanding finance, theft or a write-off record.
  • Meet at the seller’s address shown on the V5C if you can.

The paperwork

The seller should tell DVLA about the sale, and you should get the new keeper slip (the green section of the V5C). You’ll need to tax the bike before riding it; tax doesn’t transfer with the bike. You’ll also need insurance from the moment you ride it away.

Can’t ride it home?

No insurance yet, no MOT, a non-runner, or simply too far: we can collect it for you. Tell us the seller’s address, when it’s ready, and whether it runs. If you’re paying on collection, arrange the payment with the seller yourself; our driver just loads and delivers. See motorcycle transport.

FAQ

Rider questions

Can you collect a bike I bought without me being there?

Yes, if the seller is happy to hand it over. Agree payment and paperwork with them first.

Can you collect a non-running project bike?

Yes. Tell us whether the wheels turn, and send a photo if you can.
