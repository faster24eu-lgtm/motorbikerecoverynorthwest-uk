  3. What to do after a motorbike crash
  1. Get safe. Out of the road, behind a barrier or onto a verge. Warn traffic only if it’s safe to.
  2. Check for injuries. Call 999 if anyone is hurt. Don’t remove a helmet if there’s neck or back pain. Let paramedics do it.
  3. Call the police if anyone is injured, the road is blocked, the other driver leaves, or you suspect drink or drugs.
  4. Swap details: names, addresses, phone numbers, registrations and insurers. Get witness contacts too.
  5. Collect evidence. Photos of the bike, the vehicles, the road surface (diesel, gravel, potholes), signs and the view from where you were. Helmet-camera footage is gold, so save it straight away.
  6. Get the bike moved. Once the police are done, call us. We’ll recover it to your home, a dealer or storage. See accident recovery.
  7. Tell your insurer as soon as you can, even if you’re not claiming.
  8. Get checked by a doctor within a day or two, even if you feel fine. Some injuries show up later.

Your kit

A helmet that has taken a hit should be replaced, even if it looks fine. Keep damaged kit and photograph it, because it’s part of your claim.
