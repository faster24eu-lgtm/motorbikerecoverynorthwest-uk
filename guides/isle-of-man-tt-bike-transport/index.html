Isle of Man TT: getting your bike there

For a lot of North West riders, the TT is the trip of the year. Here’s how the ferries work and where we can help.

The ferries

The Isle of Man Steam Packet Company runs ferries from Heysham, near Morecambe, and Liverpool. Spaces for bikes during TT fortnight sell out far ahead, sometimes a year or more in advance, so book as early as you can.

Getting to the port

Most riders ride to the port. But if the bike has a problem the week before, or breaks down on the way, a missed ferry is expensive. We can take the bike to Heysham or the Liverpool terminal for you, and collect it when you get back. Tell us your sailing time.

If it goes wrong on the island

We don’t operate on the Isle of Man itself. If the bike breaks down there, you’ll need a local garage or recovery service on the island, and you may be able to bring the bike back on the ferry as freight. Once it’s back at Heysham or Liverpool, we can take it home.

Classic TT and the Manx Grand Prix

The same applies to the Classic TT and the Manx Grand Prix in late summer. For classic bikes, see classic motorbike transport.
