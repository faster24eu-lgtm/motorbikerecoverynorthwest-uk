The free fixes

  1. Kill switch: knocked to off. Everyone has done it.
  2. Side stand: most bikes won’t start, or cut out, in gear with the stand down.
  3. Clutch pulled in: many bikes need the clutch in to start, even in neutral.
  4. Neutral light: make sure it’s really in neutral.
  5. Fuel: the gauge can lie, especially after a long run. Is the fuel tap on, on older bikes?

If it still won’t go

  • Clicks but doesn’t turn: a flat battery or a bad connection. A jump or a push start (on a manual bike) may work.
  • Nothing at all: the main fuse, a battery terminal or the immobiliser. Try the spare key.
  • Turns over but won’t fire: fuel, spark or a sensor. Stop before you flatten the battery.
  • Starts then dies: often an immobiliser or a fuel problem.
