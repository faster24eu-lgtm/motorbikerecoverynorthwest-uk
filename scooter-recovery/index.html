For delivery riders

Most of our city scooter calls in Manchester and Liverpool are delivery riders: a seized engine after high mileage, a snapped drive belt, a flat battery or a puncture. We take the scooter to the place that can fix it today. Some riders keep a cheap spare scooter at home and swap. We can bring that one back on the same trip.

Scooters and mopeds we move

Honda PCX, Yamaha NMAX and XMAX, Vespa, Lexmoto and other 50cc–300cc scooters; learner 125s; maxi-scooters; electric mopeds; and mobility scooters (tell us the model and weight).

Stolen and recovered?

Scooters and 125s are the most stolen bikes in the North West’s cities. If police have recovered yours, the ignition and steering lock are often broken, so it can’t be ridden. See stolen bike collection.

FAQ

Rider questions

Do you move mobility scooters?

Yes, between homes, care homes and repairers. Tell us the model so we bring the right ramp, as some are very heavy.

Is scooter recovery cheaper than motorbike recovery?

Sometimes, especially for short local runs. You’ll always hear the full price before we send anyone.
