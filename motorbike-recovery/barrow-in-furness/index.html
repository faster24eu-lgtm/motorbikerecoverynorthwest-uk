Barrow, Ulverston, Dalton and Walney sit at the end of the Furness peninsula, a long way from the motorway. We recover bikes across Furness, including on the long A590 to the M6.

Where riders get stuck around Barrow

  • A590 from the M6 at junction 36: The only main road in, long and busy.
  • A595 up the West Cumbrian coast: Quiet and winding, with long gaps between villages.
  • Jubilee Bridge to Walney Island: Exposed to the wind off the sea.
  • Ulverston and Dalton town roads: Busy market town streets.

Local knowledge

Barrow is a long way from anywhere by road, and almost everything comes in on the A590. A recovery to Barrow from the M6, or from Barrow back to Lancashire, is a long run. We’ll tell you the price and the realistic arrival time on the call.

The A590 carries heavy traffic to the shipyard and the peninsula, with few safe places to stop on the dual carriageway sections. If the bike stops, get off the road and behind any barrier, and tell us the nearest village or junction.
