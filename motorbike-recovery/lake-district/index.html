Kendal, Windermere, Keswick, Penrith and over the passes: the Lakes are a bucket-list ride and a long way from a bike shop. We recover motorbikes across Cumbria, including from the high passes.

Where riders get stuck around Lake District

  • M6 junctions 36 to 40: Exposed fell sections over Shap, with crosswinds and snow in winter.
  • A591 Kendal to Keswick via Windermere: Busy with tourist traffic all summer.
  • Kirkstone, Hardknott and Wrynose passes: Steep, narrow and unforgiving to a dropped bike.
  • A66 Penrith to Keswick and A686 over Hartside: Long, fast and remote.

Local knowledge

In the Lakes, distance is everything: a recovery from Hardknott to Kendal can take longer than you expect. Signal is patchy in the valleys. Get to a village, a pub or a road with a name if you can, and send your pin or what3words the moment you have a bar. The price reflects the distance, and you hear it before we set off.

On Hardknott, Wrynose and the other steep passes, a recovery truck can’t always get to where the bike stopped. We’ll talk you through moving it to the nearest point a truck can reach, and we’ll be honest about how long it takes to get there.

FAQ

Rider questions

Can you recover a motorbike from Hardknott Pass?

Yes, though access on the steepest sections can be very tight for a recovery truck. If you can, get the bike to the foot of the pass or a flatter section. We’ll tell you where we can reach.

Can you take my bike from the Lakes back home to Manchester or Liverpool?

Yes. Long recoveries are priced by distance, and you hear the price before we set off.
