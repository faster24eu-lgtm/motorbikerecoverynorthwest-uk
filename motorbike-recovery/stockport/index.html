Stockport is where Manchester riders head for the Peak District, so a lot of our calls here come from bikes on their way to, or limping home from, the Cat and Fiddle and the A6 over to Buxton.

Where riders get stuck around Stockport

  • M60 junctions 1 to 27 around Stockport: Heavy traffic, and the bikes that overheat in it.
  • A6 through Hazel Grove and Disley: The main road to Buxton and the Peak, busy at weekends.
  • A34 and A523 towards Macclesfield: The route out to the Cat and Fiddle.
  • Stockport town centre and the pyramid: One-way systems and multi-storey car parks.

Local knowledge

On sunny weekends, the A6 and the roads out of Stockport fill up with bikes heading for the Peak. If your bike gives up halfway, a lay-by on the A6 or a pub car park is a far better place to wait than the verge on a moor. Pull in, send us your pin, and we’ll come out.
