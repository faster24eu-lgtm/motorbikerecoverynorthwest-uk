From the city centre to Salford Quays, Trafford Park and out to the M60, Manchester has more riders, more delivery scooters and more stolen bikes than anywhere else in the North West. We recover them all, 24/7.

Where riders get stuck around Manchester

  • M60 ring road: Busy lane changes and long stretches of roadworks. Get behind the barrier and call.
  • Mancunian Way and the A57(M): An elevated road with nowhere to stop. Get off at the next slip if the bike is still moving.
  • Oxford Road, Wilmslow Road and the Curry Mile: Bus lanes, students on scooters and cyclists. Lots of 125 calls.
  • Trafford Park and the Quays: Industrial roads covered in grit and debris, a classic place for punctures.

Local knowledge

Motorbike and scooter theft is a real problem in Manchester. If police have found your stolen bike, see stolen bike collection. The council has dedicated motorcycle bays at the Arndale, King Street West and Bridgewater Hall. Breakdowns in those bays and in city-centre multi-storeys are easy to load from, so tell us the bay or level.

Plenty of our Manchester calls come from commuters and delivery riders on scooters and 125s. If the bike dies in the evening rush, we can take it home or straight to a dealer for the morning. Tell us if a disc lock or alarm is fitted and where the key is; it saves time when we load.
