Birkenhead, Wallasey, West Kirby and out to Ellesmere Port: we cover the whole Wirral peninsula, from the tunnels to the coast roads.

Where riders get stuck around Wirral

  • M53 from the tunnels to Chester: The peninsula’s spine, and fast.
  • A41 New Chester Road: Busy and stop-start through Birkenhead and Bromborough.
  • Coast roads through Hoylake, West Kirby and New Brighton: Sea air and sand, so look after your chain and electrics.
  • Birkenhead tunnel approaches: Toll booths and queues.

Local knowledge

Salt and sand from the coast are hard on bikes. We see seized brake calipers, corroded connectors and chains that snap after a winter ridden near the sea. If your bike has been standing over winter by the coast, a careful check before the first ride saves a recovery call.

The coast road from New Brighton to Hoylake and the M53 down the middle of the peninsula carry most of our Wirral calls. If you’re on the promenade, give us the nearest slipway, café or car park so we can find you quickly.
