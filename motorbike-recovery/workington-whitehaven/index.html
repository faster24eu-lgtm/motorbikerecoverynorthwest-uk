Workington, Whitehaven, Maryport and Cockermouth: West Cumbria is the quiet side of the Lakes, with coast roads and the western fells on the doorstep. We recover bikes across the area.

Where riders get stuck around Workington

  • A595 along the coast: The main road north–south, busy with traffic to the coast towns.
  • A66 east from Workington to Keswick: Long climbs and fast sections into the Lakes.
  • A596 to Maryport: The coast road north.
  • Lanes to Ennerdale, Loweswater and Lorton: Quiet, narrow and beautiful.

Local knowledge

West Cumbria is a long way from the bigger recovery bases, so a breakdown here can take longer to reach than one in Kendal or Penrith. We’ll give you an honest arrival time. If you’re near a town, wait there rather than on a remote stretch.

From Cockermouth, Whinlatter Pass climbs to Keswick and the lanes lead to Lorton and Buttermere. If the bike stops on the forest road, the visitor centre car park at the top is the easiest place to wait.
